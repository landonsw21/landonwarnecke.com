About this workflow

Open a folder containing project.json and six face PNGs from cube export. Edits are saved as a small JSON file; keep it with your original cube capture. SSAA evaluates subpixel ray directions to eliminate aliasing and polygon edges. Final export samples the retained source textures, not the preview.

No uploads, external libraries, installation, or account required. Output is 8-bit PNG decoded locally by WebGL 2.
