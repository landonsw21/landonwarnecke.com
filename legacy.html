Computational Optics & KeyShot Tooling

Fisheye Studio — Continuous Ray Optical Engine

An end-to-end upgrade transforming KeyShot's perspective rendering into a calibrated physical wide-angle system. Replaces brute-force polygon duplication with single-pass continuous ray unprojection, 4× subpixel SSAA, Catmull-Rom bicubic sampling, and an offline 16K master exporter.

Drafting Software · WebGL & Canvas

Perspective Studio

A browser-based perspective drafting platform spanning 1-point up to whole-sphere 360° 6-point views. Features interactive 3D box snapping, gumball handles, subdividing depth planes, ground plane grids, and custom CAD model imports (Rhino 3DM, OBJ, STL).

Design Philosophy

Bridging the gap between physical design craft and computational toolmaking.

I am an industrial designer and software creator. Rather than treating design software as a fixed constraint, I build custom tools and rendering systems that expand how physical forms are conceived, drafted, and visualized.

My work spans 3D modeling in Rhino, custom automated pipelines in KeyShot and Python, real-time shaders in WebGL 2, and physical prototyping in the studio.
